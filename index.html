Q & A

Frequently asked questions

Which cards does Card Detective support?

Card Detective identifies baseball, basketball, football, and hockey cards, plus Pokémon, Yu-Gi-Oh!, and Magic: The Gathering.

Where do the card values come from?

Values are based on current asking prices, not sold prices. Sports cards are priced from live eBay listings. Magic: The Gathering uses Scryfall market data, and Pokémon and Yu-Gi-Oh! use TCGplayer market data, with eBay listings as the fallback.

Is Card Detective free?

Yes. You get 10 free scans every month, no account required. Card Detective Pro unlocks unlimited scans, cloud backup, and unlimited collection storage for $4.99/month or $39.99/year.

Can it estimate graded card value?

Yes. For ungraded cards, Card Detective also shows a PSA 9 graded benchmark based on current eBay asking prices for the same card in a PSA 9 holder, so you can judge whether grading is worth it.

Is my collection private?

Your collection is stored on your device. Card photos are sent securely to our server only to identify the card. No account or sign-up is required, and Pro subscribers get optional encrypted cloud backup of their collection.
