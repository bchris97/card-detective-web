Card Detective Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 15, 2026

Overview

Card Detective identifies trading cards from photos and shows what they're worth. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to have it deleted. There are no accounts: we never ask for your name, email address or Apple ID.

Information we collect

Device ID

The app creates a random identifier on your device and keeps it in your iPhone's keychain, so it survives reinstalling the app. It is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID. We use it to count your scans, to connect your Pro subscription, to store your cloud backup, and to understand how the app is used (see below).

Card photos

Photos you scan are sent to our server and to the AI service that identifies the card. We do not keep them after the scan, except card photos backed up with Pro (below).

Your collection

Cards you add are stored on your device. With Pro, your collection is also backed up to our server: card details, groups, notes, quantities, prices you entered, and a smaller copy of each card photo. This lets you restore it on a new phone.

Usage information

Linked to your device ID: how many scans you've made and whether each found a price; for Pro, a daily total of your collection's value for the Value Trend chart; and, if you turn on price alerts, which alerts were sent.

App activity

The app records the first time your device reaches certain steps — for example, opening the camera, taking a photo, getting a result, adding a card, or opening the Pro or Settings screens. Only the step and when it first happened are recorded, linked to your device ID; never what was on the screen. We use this to see where people get stuck and to improve the app. It is not used for advertising or shared with anyone.

Notifications

If you turn on price alerts, we store your device's notification token so we can send them. Turning alerts off stops them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We learn whether you have an active subscription through our subscription provider. We never receive your payment details.

Server logs

Our server keeps logs for about 7 days to fix problems. They can include your device ID and the details of cards you scanned.

How we use information

  • To identify your cards and look up their prices
  • To apply scan limits and your Pro subscription
  • To back up and restore your collection (Pro)
  • To send price alerts you've turned on
  • To find and fix problems, and to improve the app

We do not sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Who receives your information

Only the companies that run parts of the app for us. Our hosting, card identification and subscription providers are bound by data processing agreements to keep your information secure and use it only to provide their service to us. App updates come from a provider that sees only your device's IP address, iOS version and a random install ID. Notifications are delivered by Apple.

To look up prices we send card details only, never anything about you.

Keeping and deleting your information

Card photos are not kept after a scan unless backed up with Pro. Server logs are deleted after about 7 days. Cloud backups and usage information are kept until you ask us to delete them.

To have your information deleted, email us your Support ID. In the app, tap the gear icon on the Home screen, then “Contact support”, and it is filled in for you. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone.
