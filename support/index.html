Frequently asked questions

How many free scans do I get?

Free users get 10 scans per month. Pro includes 250 scans a month.

What does Pro include?

250 scans a month, cloud backup of your collection and card photos, a Value Trend chart for your collection, price history and graded prices for each card, tracking what you paid, and exporting your collection as a spreadsheet. New subscribers may be offered a 7-day free trial.

How does card identification work?

Card Detective uses AI to analyze your card photos and identify the player, year, brand, set, and condition. For sports cards, scan the front and the back: the back carries the year and card number.

Where does pricing data come from?

Sports cards are priced from current eBay listings. These are asking prices, not completed sales, so an individual sale may differ. Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering and Yu-Gi-Oh! cards use market prices from TCGplayer and Scryfall where available.

Why does my card show a different price than I see on eBay?

Prices change constantly. Card Detective filters out graded cards, lot listings, and outliers to give you the most accurate price for an ungraded single card.

My card was misidentified. What should I do?

Try scanning again in better lighting with the full card visible. For best results, lay the card flat on a plain surface, and tilt shiny cards slightly so glare doesn't cover the small print.

You can also correct any detail yourself: open the card and tap “Edit”.

How do I restore my Pro subscription?

Open Card Detective, tap the gear icon on the Home screen, and tap “Restore purchases”. Your cloud backup can be restored from the same screen.

You can also tap the Pro button and then “Restore Purchases”.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In the app, tap the gear icon on the Home screen, then “Manage subscription”. Or go to iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Card Detective.
